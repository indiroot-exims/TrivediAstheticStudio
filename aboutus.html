The studio

Material with

Trivedi Asthetic Studio began with a simple belief: the things around us should carry a little feeling. From our Ahmedabad studio, we work with resin, reclaimed timber and mineral pigments to create objects that hold light in unexpected ways.

Each piece is poured, cured, sanded and finished by hand. The process is deliberately unhurried, giving the material room to surprise us and every commission a story of its own.
